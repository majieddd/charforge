## Resume here

*Updated 2026-10-05.* Paper 0.13: faces in parts. After the shape and texture round (E132-E138, paper 0.12), faces were the fault most in view; taken one part at a time on a benchmark set of two characters per style and gender (E140), each had its own cause and fix - eyes the generator left as openings full of shards rebuilt as a cap in the solid, the head keeping 28% of the triangles, the picture's face laid on part by part onto the model's own features (not on anime), the face left to the front view and out of the skin tone, the generator's colour rebuilt through its mesh (the crackle). Realistic faces gained most; drawn faces held. Left: the hairline, unread anime eyes and the profile (E141), shape faults (E139).

Build a character: `python charforge.py make --prompt "..." --name x` (or the Studio: `python charforge.py studio`). Score a move from video: `python tools/motion_fidelity.py --name <n> --move <m>` and `python tools/motion_stages.py --name <n> --move <m>`. See it from every side: `python tools/make_angles.py --name <n> --clip <m> --video`. Rebuild the paper after an experiment: `python paper/collect.py && python paper/build.py`.

## How to add an experiment

1. Add an entry to `research/experiments.json` with the next free id (`E1xx` for the current programme), status `planned`, and its question, method and metric - before running it.
2. Run it; record the measurement in `result` with the file it came from, and the `decision`.
3. If it changes a number the paper quotes, re-run `python paper/collect.py`; then `python paper/build.py`, add a line to the changelog in `paper/meta.json`, and commit the code, data and paper together.

Hunyuan3D-Paint 2.1 on the cut-out head (blender/cut_head.py) with the face close-up as its reference paints views that sit on the geometry by construction, but at the 256 px the port can afford here the face comes out posterised, the eyes as blobs - worse than the projected picture. Hunyuan3D 2.1's shape model on the head crop works where TRELLIS.2's did not: 7.5 minutes, a sculpted face - brows, lids, lips, ears - that follows the picture (Boyscout, raking light). From the front and three-quarter it reads well; at the side the edge of the front projection shows on the cheek and the back of the head has little detail.

### E140

**Faces in parts: eyes, triangles, features and skin, each on its own** · texture · done · 2026-10-05

- **Question.** Faces read as sloppy - eyes smeared or hollow, features not where the shape has them, patches on the cheek - though the body's shape and texture are now close to a professional model's (E132-E138). Taken one part at a time, what makes each part of a face clear, and which faults belong to which part?
- **Method.** Each part of the face judged on its own in soft-light close-ups (tools/face_review.sh) and by renders of the shape alone, the generator's paint and the shipped texture in the same face frame (blender/render_front.py --frame face): texel density; the eyes' geometry at every stage (generated mesh, solid, decimated low-poly); the head's share of the triangle budget; where the picture's features land against the model's own (DWPose's 68 points); which source paints each patch of the face (the front view alone, with the side views, before and after the skin tone). A benchmark set of two characters per style and gender (Mara, Juno, Rowan, Knight; Vex, Rivet, Pip, Cadet; Aoi, Hana, Kaito, Ren) instead of the whole roster, scored before and after by a face score: landmark error against the picture after one similarity (NME, by eye spacing), DWPose's confidence, and per-part likeness from DINOv3 features of the render laid onto the picture (tools/face_score.py, tools/face_likeness.py).
- **Metric.** Face NME and per-part DINOv3 likeness (face, eyes, nose, mouth) before and after on the benchmark set; close-ups by eye; the E132 shape audit and the aberration and foot audits unchanged or better.
- **Result.** Texel density was not the bottleneck: 2.5x the texels across the head (--head-texels) showed no difference at review size. The eyes were: TRELLIS.2 models every eye as an almond opening with shards inside it (all seven characters looked at), the solid filled it with streaks and collapse decimation, which gave the head 17% of 60k triangles, crumpled it into a pit - no texture laid on a pit reads as an eye. Rebuilt in the solid (pipeline/solidify.py --eyes from blender/eye_fill.py and pipeline/eye_marks.py) as a cap across the opening that follows the lids' depth at 16 points round the rim, bulges a little forward and creases a little at the rim, the region smoothed and kept from being put back onto the shards over a feathered zone larger than the marked eye (at the marked size the upper lid's shards came back as pleats; a hard edge showed as a step). Two earlier versions failed: a whole sphere put in the mesh was voxelised whole and bulged out past the corners, and a ball set behind the opening left a slot whose hard ledges rendered as dark goggle rings on the shipped rig. The head is held at 28% of the triangles (blender/retopo.py --head-share, a vertex group whose strength is found by bisection). The eyes are found by DWPose on the generator's own paint, which lies where its geometry is (0.97 on Mara; on a grey render of a drawn face DWPose only guessed - it put Pip's mouth under his nose); anime eyes it cannot read (Aoi 0.46) are left as generated, after the picture's points carried over by silhouette and pits found in the depth both failed. The features: the projection's silhouette fit and face flow had put Mara's painted face larger than her sculpted one, eyes 10-30 px off, squinting and smeared; laying the face on in parts - each brow, eye, the nose, mouth and jaw moved by its own similarity onto the model's own points, spread as a smooth field that falls to nothing past the face - replaces the face flow and gives her the picture's face. The patches: the generated side and back views blended in where the front sees the face at a slant (fixed by leaving the face's ellipse to the front view), and the skin tone (E138) cut the picture's soft cheek shadow into patches with its colour bands (the face is now left as painted). The generator's own texture is 1024 px for a million faces, most triangles smaller than a texel, with texels sampled off the surface along its charts' edges - a crackle of grey and dark lines on every surface the picture does not paint. Not holes (a white render has none) and not dark specks (a 2D speck filter missed most); mended from atlas neighbours, tiny triangles took other charts' colours. Rebuilt through the mesh (pipeline/despeck_source.py: vertex colours as the median of their triangles', outliers to their neighbours', the atlas painted again at 4x): clean skin and hair. New characters are generated at 2048 px. Drawn smiles read 1.2 eye-spacings wide and the jaw's cut drew a line across Cadet's face: held to 0.8. Anime is not laid on in parts: a drawn face's shape does not say how big its eyes are, and fitted to the generator's paint Kaito's eyes came out a fifth wider than his picture's. On the benchmark set (10 with a v0.12 build to compare), median likeness of the eyes 0.63 -> 0.70 (8 better), whole face 0.67 -> 0.72 (8 better), mouth 0.53 -> 0.63 (9 better), nose 0.64 -> 0.68, landmark error 3.2% -> 2.8%. The gain is the realistic faces' (eyes 0.51 -> 0.62, face 0.59 -> 0.67); stylised (0.72 -> 0.71) and anime (0.82 -> 0.83) held. Shape, crushed faces and foot slip held (noise 2.62 -> 2.59 deg, crushed 1.15 -> 0.97%, slip unchanged); frames with a part deep in another moved -3.6 to +11.9 points a character, Vex's +11.9 at the 2 cm line (her first rebuild this round: 45.9 against 46.4).
- **Decision.** Defaults in v0.13: --eyes rebuild (a cap across each opening read on the generator's paint), --head-share 0.28, the face laid on in parts when both faces read and the style is not anime (it replaces the face flow), the face's ellipse painted by the front view alone and left out of the skin tone, the generator's colour rebuilt through the mesh before the bake, TRELLIS.2's texture at 2048, the mouth cut held to the lips (a mouth read wider than the eye spacing opens 0.6 of it), opt-in build options remembered per character. Open (E141): the hairline, anime eyes the pose model cannot read, the face from the side; shape faults (Vex, Ren) are E139's.
- **Files.** `blender/eye_fill.py`, `pipeline/eye_marks.py`, `pipeline/solidify.py`, `blender/sdf_io.py`, `blender/retopo.py`, `pipeline/project_texture.py`, `pipeline/skin_tone.py`, `pipeline/despeck_source.py`, `blender/face_rig.py`, `blender/render_front.py`, `tools/face_review.sh`, `tools/face_score.py`, `tools/face_likeness.py`, `charforge_notes/polish/faces/e140/NOTES.md`, `charforge_notes/polish/v013_rebuild.sh`
