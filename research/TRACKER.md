Build a character: `python charforge.py make --prompt "..." --name x` (or the Studio: `python charforge.py studio`). Score a move from video: `python tools/motion_fidelity.py --name <n> --move <m>` and `python tools/motion_stages.py --name <n> --move <m>`. See it from every side: `python tools/make_angles.py --name <n> --clip <m> --video`. Rebuild the paper after an experiment: `python paper/collect.py && python paper/build.py`.

## How to add an experiment

1. Add an entry to `research/experiments.json` with the next free id (`E1xx` for the current programme), status `planned`, and its question, method and metric - before running it.
2. Run it; record the measurement in `result` with the file it came from, and the `decision`.
3. If it changes a number the paper quotes, re-run `python paper/collect.py`; then `python paper/build.py`, add a line to the changelog in `paper/meta.json`, and commit the code, data and paper together.

## Details

### E001

**Motion from a video by retrieval: match the pose sequence against the Mixamo library** · motion · done · 2026-09-24

- **Question.** Can a clip from a 2,453-clip capture library stand in for the motion a video shows, found by its 2D projection alone?
- **Method.** ViTPose on each frame; dynamic time warping over 16 camera angles, mirrored or not, against every clip's projected joints.
- **Metric.** Self-test: 40 library clips from random angles with pose noise, a 3 s stretch and a speed change. Right clip (or an exact duplicate) first; camera angle error.
- **Result.** Right clip or a duplicate first in 40 of 40 (top-1 exactly 0.675, the rest ties with duplicates); angle off by 5.4 deg (median). results/v3/fit_selftest_left_out.json
- **Decision.** Kept as the first step of every move from video.
- **Files.** `pipeline/video_motion.py`, `blender/motion_library.py`

### E002

**Bend the retrieved capture toward the video (the fit)** · motion · done · 2026-09-24

- **Question.** When the library lacks the move, how much of the gap can a per-bone fit close?
- **Method.** Each bone's image direction from the video, its depth from its length; keep the capture where it agrees within the pose model's noise; blend whole bones; smooth only the correction; fit only when the capture misses by more than 1.2x the jitter.
- **Metric.** 3D pose error against the true motion, torso lengths, 40 held-out moves.
- **Result.** Current self-test (results/v3/fit_selftest_*.json, 40 moves each): left out of the library 0.269 -> 0.189 with the capture's bone lengths, 0.212 with the video's own (E003); in the library 0.031 -> 0.024. An earlier run of the test gave 0.269 -> 0.178, of which 0.143 was depth. Depth from the best of four captures: worse (dropped).
- **Decision.** Kept.
- **Files.** `pipeline/video_motion.py`

### E003

**Fit on the video's own body, not the capture's** · motion · done · 2026-09-24

- **Question.** Do the capture actor's bone lengths bend a short-torsoed character into a lean?
- **Method.** Measure each bone only where the capture shows it flat to the camera, shrink toward the capture's length by the estimate's error.
- **Metric.** The fit's image error, torso lengths (Aoi); the self-test with the video's body the capture actor's own, and another.
- **Result.** Aoi 0.061 -> 0.028, the 28 deg lean back gone. Self-test: on another body (the real case) 0.324 -> 0.259 with the video's own lengths against 0.311 with the capture's; on the capture actor's own body the capture's lengths are better (0.189 against 0.212) - as they must be.
- **Decision.** Kept.
- **Files.** `pipeline/video_motion.py`

### E005

**Refine the rig against the video (analysis by synthesis)** · motion · done · 2026-09-24

- **Question.** How much closer does optimising the character's own skinned mesh against the video's silhouette get?
- **Method.** PyTorch forward kinematics mirroring Blender's, the skinned mesh projected with the video's camera; 20 bones a frame nudged against silhouette chamfer, joints, face and a prior.
- **Metric.** Joints (torso lengths) and silhouette IoU, four moves.
- **Result.** +0.07-0.10 IoU on every move; now joints 0.054-0.118, IoU 0.77-0.84 (paper, Table 3).
- **Decision.** Kept.
- **Files.** `pipeline/refine_pose.py`, `blender/apply_pose_corrections.py`

### E102

**An aberration score for every clip of every character** · measurement · open · 2026-09-30

- **Question.** Which geometry and motion defects can be measured reproducibly on the exact surface frames and which require a separate truth or image-based test?
- **Method.** Before interpreting scores, run synthetic clean controls and injected defects. Static source review confirms the script now asserts evaluated loop-triangle identity/order before reusing rest masks; computes the center-sample local-shape chord residual ||Lmid-(Lprev+Lnext)/2||/mean-rest-edge and reports that center frame; renders the worst sampled frame for each implemented measure; and refuses existing output paths unless --force is explicit. Then compare Bo idle, Aoi idle, Bo crouch_walk, Aoi crouch_walk and Knight2's worst existing pose under matched DQS/LBS schedules. Surface samples use the exact --step frame list; BVH/exposure uses its separately reported --bvh-step list (--through-step is a compatibility alias). BVHTree overlap plus a rest-centroid filter >--apart yields broad-phase candidates only—not triangle intersections, surface distance or penetration. The reported normalized candidate field divides by all possible triangle pairs (nF choose 2), although eligibility requires two live faces; disclose both the raw counts and this denominator. Texture drift, runtime face morphs and contact-phase penetration need distinct measures. Calibrate geometry, texture and motion-plausibility dimensions on blinded, randomized, style-stratified examples including score-boundary cases; use at least two raters, report the ICC model/type and uncertainty clustered by character/clip, not frame. Do not use any proxy as a release gate before validation.
- **Metric.** Face-count shares for area/normal flags by sampled frame; sparse BVH candidate counts with exact frame list; raw candidate pairs eligible under the live-face filter and the code's per-million normalization using all possible pairs (nF choose 2), explicitly noting there is no live-pair denominator; ray-exposed flagged-triangle-sample rates with numerator and denominator; fixed-plane vertex counts; center-frame pop vertex count and event-window rate among n-2 eligible windows. Separately report injected-defect recall/clean false positives, per-dimension human agreement and grouped uncertainty. Never label BVH candidates as intersections or normal rays as camera visibility.
- **Result.** Validated on a synthetic character with aberrations of known size (tools/aberration_controls.py): 22/22 checks pass under DQS and LBS - every measure zero when still; crushed and stretched exactly the 12.30% of triangles built to be; floor -3.0 cm; a one-frame pop found on its own frame and a fast smooth bend not flagged; penetrations of 0.5/1/2/5 cm measured as 0.50/1.00/2.00/5.00 cm. DQS vs LBS (surface): crushed 2.72% vs 3.51%, stretched 1.52% vs 1.32%. Step 1 costs ~20% over step 2 and cannot miss a one-frame pop; now the default.
- **Decision.** Adopted as the rig's QA (tools/aberrations.py -> work/<n>/qa/aberrations.json; contact code shared with joint limits in blender/mesh_contact.py). Penetration is the actionable measure (E128 acts on it). Still open: agreement with blinded human ratings before any score is used as a gate; texture drift and face morphs need their own measures.
- **Files.** `blender/aberration_audit.py`, `blender/aberration_controls.py`, `blender/mesh_contact.py`, `notes/aberration-visibility-handoff.md`, `research/data/e102_controls.json`, `tools/aberration_controls.py`, `tools/aberrations.py`

### E128

**Joint limits from each character's own mesh** · animation · done · 2026-09-30

- **Question.** Can a character's own mesh tell how far its elbows, knees and arms may turn before one limb sinks into another, and does holding every clip inside those limits remove penetration without breaking anything else?
- **Method.** blender/joint_limits.py bends each elbow and knee from rest about its hinge axis (read from the character's clips) and lowers each arm from the T-pose at five swings, measuring depth with the E102 contact code at each 5 deg step; a limit is the last angle before 2 cm (the audit's 'deep'; 1 cm opened Mara's boxing guard by 25 deg).
