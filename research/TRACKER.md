Build a character: `python charforge.py make --prompt "..." --name x` (or the Studio: `python charforge.py studio`). Score a move from video: `python tools/motion_fidelity.py --name <n> --move <m>` and `python tools/motion_stages.py --name <n> --move <m>`. See it from every side: `python tools/make_angles.py --name <n> --clip <m> --video`. Rebuild the paper after an experiment: `python paper/collect.py && python paper/build.py`.

## How to add an experiment

1. Add an entry to `research/experiments.json` with the next free id (`E1xx` for the current programme), status `planned`, and its question, method and metric - before running it.
2. Run it; record the measurement in `result` with the file it came from, and the `decision`.
3. If it changes a number the paper quotes, re-run `python paper/collect.py`; then `python paper/build.py`, add a line to the changelog in `paper/meta.json`, and commit the code, data and paper together.

### E102

**An aberration score for every clip of every character** · measurement · open · 2026-09-30

- **Question.** Which geometry and motion defects can be measured reproducibly on the exact surface frames and which require a separate truth or image-based test?
- **Method.** Before interpreting scores, run synthetic clean controls and injected defects. Static source review confirms the script now asserts evaluated loop-triangle identity/order before reusing rest masks; computes the center-sample local-shape chord residual ||Lmid-(Lprev+Lnext)/2||/mean-rest-edge and reports that center frame; renders the worst sampled frame for each implemented measure; and refuses existing output paths unless --force is explicit. Then compare Bo idle, Aoi idle, Bo crouch_walk, Aoi crouch_walk and Knight2's worst existing pose under matched DQS/LBS schedules. The audit measures live evaluated loop-triangle area ratio (<0.5 crushed, >2 stretched), a >60 degree face-normal deviation from its dominant bone's rest-normal rotation, vertices below fixed world z=-0.01 m (not contact-conditioned), and sparse single-normal-ray exposure. Surface samples use the exact --step frame list; BVH/exposure uses its separately reported --bvh-step list (--through-step is a compatibility alias). BVHTree overlap plus a rest-centroid filter >--apart yields broad-phase candidates only—not triangle intersections, surface distance or penetration. The reported normalized candidate field divides by all possible triangle pairs (nF choose 2), although eligibility requires two live faces; disclose both the raw counts and this denominator. The pop threshold defaults to 0.5 mean-rest-edge, with events over n-2 eligible windows of duration 2*step/fps; this is cadence-specific displacement, not physical jerk. Use --step=1 and --bvh-step=1 as the temporal reference, then compare cheaper schedules against injected one-frame pops and brief intersections; report defect recall and clean-control false positives. Texture drift, runtime face morphs and contact-phase penetration need distinct measures. Calibrate geometry, texture and motion-plausibility dimensions on blinded, randomized, style-stratified examples including score-boundary cases; use at least two raters, report the ICC model/type and uncertainty clustered by character/clip, not frame. Do not use any proxy as a release gate before validation.
- **Metric.** Face-count shares for area/normal flags by sampled frame; sparse BVH candidate counts with exact frame list; raw candidate pairs eligible under the live-face filter and the code's per-million normalization using all possible pairs (nF choose 2), explicitly noting there is no live-pair denominator; ray-exposed flagged-triangle-sample rates with numerator and denominator; fixed-plane vertex counts; center-frame pop vertex count and event-window rate among n-2 eligible windows. Separately report injected-defect recall/clean false positives, per-dimension human agreement and grouped uncertainty. Never label BVH candidates as intersections or normal rays as camera visibility.
- **Result.** Validated on a synthetic character with aberrations of known size (tools/aberration_controls.py): 22/22 checks pass under DQS and LBS - every measure zero when still; crushed and stretched exactly the 12.30% of triangles built to be; floor -3.0 cm; a one-frame pop found on its own frame and a fast smooth bend not flagged; penetrations of 0.5/1/2/5 cm measured as 0.50/1.00/2.00/5.00 cm. The controls caught three errors, now fixed: quads re-split by pose (triangles now fixed at rest), a ring search that capped depth at 3.1 cm, and false 'inside' readings at open region rims and between layers (Aoi's forearm 15.9 cm; Vex's elbow 15.7 cm at 105 deg, 0 at 115) - a vertex is inside only if it projects straight onto the face and a ray from it leaves through the far wall. BVHTree.overlap is a triangle-triangle test (two-triangle check), so its pairs are real intersections; depth separates contact from penetration. Roster (13 characters, every frame, DQS, before clearance): crushed 2.72%, stretched 1.52%, sheared 2.71% of faces; frames with >2 cm penetration 48.44% between body regions (49.76% counting hair); deepest body penetration 3.89 cm (clip mean). DQS vs LBS (surface): crushed 2.72% vs 3.51%, stretched 1.52% vs 1.32%. Step 1 costs ~20% over step 2 and cannot miss a one-frame pop; now the default.
- **Decision.** Adopted as the rig's QA (tools/aberrations.py -> work/<n>/qa/aberrations.json; contact code shared with joint limits in blender/mesh_contact.py). Penetration is the actionable measure (E128 acts on it). Still open: agreement with blinded human ratings before any score is used as a gate; texture drift and face morphs need their own measures.
- **Files.** `blender/aberration_audit.py`, `blender/aberration_controls.py`, `blender/mesh_contact.py`, `notes/aberration-visibility-handoff.md`, `research/data/e102_controls.json`, `tools/aberration_controls.py`, `tools/aberrations.py`

- **Question.** 27% of animation frames have a body part more than 2 cm inside another, almost all arms and hands in the torso and thighs. Does a retargeter that models the body's own surface (MeshRet) remove that without breaking contacts or feet?
