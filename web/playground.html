Generated from a text prompt on a laptop — geometry, skeleton and skinning all local. Geometry from stochastic multi-view conditioning, retopologised to a watertight quad mesh with 4K albedo and normal baked from the high-resolution surface. Mixamo capture retargeted onto a T-posed rig, skinned throughout — the way a game character actually ships. Deformation uses dual quaternion skinning: glTF carries one weight set and viewers apply it linearly, which hollows out the inside of every bent joint. Blending the rotations as rotations instead takes jacket faces collapsed below half their rest area from 8.8% to 6.1% over the walk and 11.5% to 6.0% over the run. Toggle it to see the difference.
